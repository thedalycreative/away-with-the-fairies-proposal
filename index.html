A Podcast Proposal for RTRFM 92.1

Away With The Fairies

4 Decades. 2 Hosts. 1 Isolated City.
Perth's Untold Queer History

The Concept

While the world was rioting at Stonewall or marching through the streets of Sydney, what was happening in Perth?

Away With The Fairies is a four-part oral history series hosted by Tim Daly and Joe Cassidy. We explore the "Perth Time Dilation"—the unique gap between global queer liberation and the local reality of the world's most isolated capital city.

From the illegal underground whispers of the 70s to the global drag explosions of today, we sit down with the elders, the icons, and the witnesses who lived through the evolution of Perth's gay male experience.

The Vibe: Intimate, raw, and unapologetically queer. Think NPR's Rough Translation meets Radiolab with the sharp wit of Las Culturistas. We're not here for sanitized history—we're here for the tea, the trauma, and the triumph.

The Sound: Cinematic audio storytelling with layered archival audio, ambient soundscapes from Perth's queer venues (past and present), and a Hi-NRG pulse that honors the dance floors that kept us alive. Each episode features a curated soundtrack spanning from Sylvester to SOPHIE, weaving music into the narrative fabric.

The Hosts: Tim and Joe bring the dynamic of a long-term relationship—playful banter, genuine curiosity, and the occasional loving disagreement. We're welcoming "a third" into our relationship for each episode: a guest who lived the decade we're exploring. It's confessional, conversational, and deeply personal.

The Series Arc

①
The 1970s

The Underground

Proposed Thirds:
  • Graham Douglas
  • Brian Greig
  • Ron Ames
  • Rodney Croome
  • Keith Bennett

The era of "The Camp." While the US saw liberation, Perth saw secrecy. We discuss the founding of CAMP WA, the hidden house parties, and the birth of a community that had to be invisible to survive.

②
The 1980s

The Fight & The Art

Proposed Thirds:
  • Strykermeyer
  • David Whish-Wilson
  • WAAC (AIDS Council)
  • Court Hotel Legends
  • WA AIDS Activists

The decade of contrast. The vibrant explosion of high art and performance clashing with the grim reality of the AIDS crisis. A look at how Perth's isolation both hurt and helped the community band together.

③
The 1990s

The Party & The Law

Proposed Thirds:
  • Matt McFarlane (BarbieQ)
  • DJ Timbee
  • Matthew Landers (Val Nourished)
  • DJ Drew Green
  • Pride WA Founders

Decriminalisation finally hits WA in 1990—decades late. We explore the "Golden Era" of The Court Hotel, the rise of dance culture, and the shift from political fighting to celebrating in the streets.

④
Now & Future

The Global Stage

All-Star Group-Session / Sex Party Guests:
  • Troye Sivan
  • Narelda Jacobs
  • Joel Creasey
  • Hannah Conda
  • Scarlet Adams
  • Georges LeBar / RuPaul

A high-impact finale featuring diverse Perth icons who have gone national or global. We discuss the Perth-to-World pipeline, how digital connection ended the isolation, and what it means to be a queer Western Australian on the main stage today.

Why Choose Us

2012: Tim Daly, alongside Tim Lanzon (DJ Timbee), Ser Camileri, Paul Hunt and Graham [Unknown] outside RTRFM's Studios in Mt Lawley during an interview with Graeme Watson for OutInPerth Magazine about ATQ's "Terrible Two's" Birthday!

Tim Daly was one of the founders of All Things Queer on RTRFM and started as the show's Content Director with Paul Hunt. Tim brought a vibrant energy and connected everyone whether it be host or listener—he was always breathing life into the youth of the show.

Joe Cassidy was a regular host for ATQ from the time of its inception in 2009/2010 until 2015 and was known for his gripping ability to tackle the deeper and more serious side of the gay community's news and storytelling.

Together they might be unstoppable!

We are proposing this as a high-value digital-first series to be hosted on the RTRFM website for on-demand streaming, offering premium content for the station's digital audience.

Flexible Format

4 x 45-minute episodes with potential for more—Perth has many untold queer tales waiting to be shared. Each episode is a standalone experience while contributing to a complete narrative arc.

Zero Studio Impact

Pre-recorded outside business hours. All recording and editing handled by The Daly Creative. Delivered as broadcast-ready audio files—no disruption to your regular programming schedule.

Strategic Synergy

We aim to work alongside All Things Queer. Short promo interviews on ATQ drive traffic to the RTRFM website/podcast feed, boosting digital engagement.

Lasting Impact

Create a high-quality digital archive of Perth's queer history under the RTRFM banner. Premium content that showcases the station's commitment to community storytelling.

The Story Perth Needs to Hear

Why Now?

Our elders are aging. Their stories—raw, unfiltered, and vital—risk being lost forever. Away With The Fairies captures these voices before they fade, creating a permanent record of resilience, joy, and survival.

Why RTRFM?

For decades, RTRFM has been the voice of Perth's underground. From All Things Queer to countless other community programs, you've championed stories that mainstream media ignores. This series is a natural evolution of that legacy.

The Impact

This isn't just a podcast—it's community preservation. It's education. It's a digital monument to Perth's queer pioneers. And it positions RTRFM as the definitive home for Western Australian LGBTQIA+ storytelling.

Four decades of silence. Four episodes to break it.

Let's bring Perth's queer history out of the shadows—together.
